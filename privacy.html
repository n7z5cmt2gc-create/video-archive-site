Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

About the Application

Video Archive is a personal video management application operated locally on the owner's Mac. It is not available to the general public.

Google User Data

The application uses Google OAuth to access the owner's YouTube account for uploading approved videos and managing playlists.

Use of Data

YouTube access is used only to upload videos approved by the owner, set their visibility, and add videos to designated playlists.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and upload records are stored locally on the owner's Mac. Credentials are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

Approved videos are transmitted to YouTube. Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored credentials and records are retained until deleted by the owner. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For inquiries, contact the application owner using the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
